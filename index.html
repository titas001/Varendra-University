How AI works · 3 of 5 · How a chatbot writes

Like your phone keyboard, a chatbot guesses the next word, again and again.

Scores for the next word

1Read the sentence so far.

2Give every possible next word a score.

3Pick the best one, add it, repeat.

এখন যা হচ্ছে

Scores are illustrative. Real models choose among tens of thousands of word pieces (“tokens”).

Project 1 · BRAC Health Mentor Sim

Health workers practise difficult conversations with an AI patient before they meet real ones.

The problem

New community health workers learn mostly on real patients, where a missed danger sign can cost a life.

How it works
1The AI plays a patient in Bangla, with a realistic health problem.
2The health worker asks questions and gives advice, as in a real visit.
3The AI scores the conversation and explains what to improve.
Practice session · Fever case

AI feedback 8 / 10
